Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a delicious Italian dish made with layers of pasta, seasoned ground beef, tomato sauce, and melted cheese. It is baked until golden and served warm. It's perfect for sharing with family and friends!

Ingredients

  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the ground beef in a pan.
  3. Add the tomato sauce and mix well.
  4. Place layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese in a baking dish.
  5. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
  6. Bake for 30–40 minutes until the cheese is golden.
  7. Let it cool for a few minutes before serving.
