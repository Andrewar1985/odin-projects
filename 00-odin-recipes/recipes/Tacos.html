Tacos

Description

Tacos are a traditional Mexican dish made with soft or crispy tortillas filled with meat, vegetables, and cheese. They are often served with salsa, lime, and fresh cilantro. They are tasty and easy to enjoy!

Ingredients

  • Ground beef
  • Corn tortillas
  • Lettuce

Steps

  1. Heat a pan over medium heat.
  2. Cook the ground beef until fully cooked.
  3. Warm the corn tortillas in a pan.
  4. Wash and cut the lettuce.
  5. Place the beef inside each tortilla.
  6. Add the lettuce on top.
  7. Serve and enjoy your tacos!
