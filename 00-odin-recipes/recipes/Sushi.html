Sushi

Description

Sushi is a popular Japanese dish made with rice, fresh fish, and vegetables. It is often served with soy sauce, wasabi, and pickled ginger. It is delicious and full of flavor!

Ingredients

  • Sushi rice
  • Fresh salmon
  • Nori (seaweed)

Steps

  1. Wash the sushi rice with cold water.
  2. Cook the rice according to the package instructions.
  3. Let the rice cool down.
  4. Place a sheet of nori on a bamboo mat.
  5. Spread the rice over the nori.
  6. Add cooked salmon and roll carefully.
  7. Cut the roll into small pieces and serve.
